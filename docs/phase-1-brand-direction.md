# SaltNPepper brand direction

- Text-only wordmark: bold geometric `SaltNPepper`, with the `N` as the paprika accent.
- Compact identity: `SNP`.
- Palette: charcoal `#1C1917`, paprika `#B43A25`, ivory `#F7F2E8`, paper `#FFFDF8`.
- Typography: Archivo Black for display/wordmark and DM Sans for interface/body copy.
- Tone: modern, local, warm, direct, and cuisine-neutral until the real menu is supplied.
- Photography: editorial stock is temporary and must never be described as actual restaurant dishes or premises.
- UI: shared cards, forms, dialogs, focus treatment, and minimum 44px targets across public, admin, and Android.
- Destructive actions stay separated from routine actions and require confirmation where applicable.
